Raised on a rural homestead.

Our mini cream Dachshunds are lovingly raised on our 6-acre rural homestead in La Grange, California. Nestled among gently rolling hills covered in golden grass and dotted with majestic oak trees, this serene environment provides the perfect backdrop for healthy, happy puppies to grow.

Plenty of outdoor play

Our dogs enjoy plenty of outdoor play on our 6-acre homestead. Our mama dogs each have their own private whelping space with free, independent access to the outdoors, ensuring everyone gets fresh air and exercise in a safe setting.

Natural socialization & family care

From day one, our puppies receive natural socialization and family care from our three children, who help them become well-adjusted. This hands-on, family-oriented approach prepares them to be loving companions for your home.

Adoption Journey

Step 1: Choose a Puppy

Fill out the Adoption Application when you’ve chosen a puppy and are ready to reserve, or sign up for our free Notification List if you want alerts when a new litter arrives.

Step 2: Reserve

We review the application. If approved, you get the purchase agreement + Square invoice. You have 24 hours to complete. Reserved = signed contract and paid $1,000 deposit.

Step 3: Get Your Puppy

Updates about every two weeks on Available Puppies. Pickup by appointment in La Grange, or an approved delivery. Balance due at pickup, or two business days before any delivery.

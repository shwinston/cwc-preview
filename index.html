Clover raised

Ingredients for a happy pup.

  1. Raised on a rural homestead.

    Our mini cream Dachshunds are lovingly raised on our 6-acre rural homestead in La Grange, California. Nestled among gently rolling hills covered in golden grass and dotted with majestic oak trees, this serene environment provides the perfect backdrop for healthy, happy puppies to grow.

  2. Plenty of outdoor play

    Our dogs enjoy plenty of outdoor play on our 6-acre homestead. Our mama dogs each have their own private whelping space with free, independent access to the outdoors, ensuring everyone gets fresh air and exercise in a safe setting.

  3. Natural socialization & family care

    From day one, our puppies receive natural socialization and family care from our three children, who help them become well-adjusted. This hands-on, family-oriented approach prepares them to be loving companions for your home.

Good to know

Questions families ask.

How much is a puppy?

$3,600 for black & cream and $3,800 for clear or shaded cream (pet price, AKC Limited Registration).

How do I reserve?

Choose a puppy by name on Available Puppies, then submit the Adoption Application. If approved, you get the purchase agreement and a $1,000 deposit invoice; the puppy is reserved once both are completed within 24 hours. The deposit is credited toward the price.

When do puppies go home?

Each litter has a published pickup date at 8 weeks of age. Remaining balance is due at pickup.

Pickup or delivery?

Pickup by appointment in La Grange, about 50 minutes east of Modesto. Delivery can be arranged when approved in advance: airport drop-off (SFO, Oakland, Sacramento, Fresno) is $300 for a travel nanny, and ground delivery and flight nanny trips are quoted individually. See all options.

Is there a health guarantee?

Yes. A one-year health guarantee for congenital or hereditary defects and 15 days for infectious disease, with a vet exam required within 72 hours of pickup. Full terms and exclusions are in Our Care Promise.

What comes home with my puppy?

Every puppy goes home with a starter kit: a blanket, a bag of food, a water/food bowl, a dog toy, potty pads, and our business card.

Is there a waitlist?

We do not run a years-long numbered waitlist. Join the free Notification List for alerts, or place a $1,000 Future Litter Reservation if no current puppy fits. It is refundable until a suitable puppy has been offered.

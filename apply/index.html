Adopt your puppy

Adoption Application.

We place a limited number of puppies each year and personally review every application. Not every application is approved. This application is for families who have chosen a specific puppy on the Available Puppies page and are ready to reserve.

  • Puppies are reserved on a completed-packet basis, not on who submitted the form first.
  • A puppy is officially reserved only after we have received the signed Puppy Purchase Agreement and the $1,000 deposit.
  • By submitting, you confirm that if we approve you, you intend to sign the agreement and pay the $1,000 deposit within 24 hours of our approval email.
  • The $1,000 deposit is credited toward the purchase price ($3,600 black & cream; $3,800 clear or shaded cream).

Questions? Call, text, or email contact@creamwithclover.com. Hours: Sunday–Thursday 7am–7pm; Friday 7am–4pm; Saturday closed.
